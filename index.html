Starship HLS

1 / 2 / 3 or R / F (Xbox LB / RB, touch ENG +/−) choose how many of the three inner Raptors fire. With the guidance flying, that choice sets the braking engines (or the landing-burn engines once the ship has pitched up). In manual it is the live engine count. T switches the throttle between manual and the hoverslam autothrottle, X (Xbox X) cuts the Raptors off or starts them, at any time and in either mode. A shutdown takes about 0.4 s and still delivers a short tail-off impulse, and a start takes about 2 s to reach minimum throttle, so time a relight early.

Even one Raptor at its 40 % minimum pushes the landed ship at about twice lunar gravity, so Starship cannot hover: every landing ends in a constant-deceleration “hoverslam” timed to reach 1 m/s at the pads.
